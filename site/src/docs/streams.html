The Streams screen lists every JetStream stream in the active Context's account. Open one to see its state, config, consumers and messages.

Find a stream

Type a subject in the filter, such as orders.> or orders.*.new, to see only the streams that store it. Each row shows the stream's subjects, storage, replicas, message count, size, consumers and last activity, with a health dot.

A stream's page

A stream has four tabs:

  • Overview: messages, size, first and last sequence and time, deleted messages, subjects, consumers, storage, largest message allowed, replicas, sources and mirrors, and a tree of its subjects with counts.
  • Config: the stream's full configuration.
  • Consumers: its consumers and their lag. Manage them from Consumers.
  • Messages: the Message browser for this stream.

Create a stream

Choose New stream on the Streams screen. The form covers the settings you use most:

SettingNotes
Name, description, subjectsOne subject per line; * and > wildcards are allowed.
Storage, retentionFile or memory; limits, interest or work queue. Fixed once created.
When full, discardOld or new messages.
Replicas1, 3 or 5. More than one needs a cluster.
Max messages, bytes, age, message size, messages per subject, consumersEmpty means unlimited (or the server's default).
Duplicate windowHow long repeated Nats-Msg-Id headers are dropped.
Allow direct get, allow roll-ups, deny delete, deny purgeOn or off.

Durations take values like 30s, 15m, 24h or 7d. Anything the server refuses is shown on the field it's about. Below the form is the nats stream add command that does the same.

Edit, purge and delete

The stream's header has Edit, Purge… and Delete…. Editing keeps any setting the form doesn't show (sources, mirrors, placement…) as it is.

Purge removes messages but keeps the stream and its consumers. Choose what to remove:

  • every message;
  • messages on one subject;
  • everything except the newest N;
  • everything before a sequence.

Delete removes the stream, its messages and its consumers. Both ask you to type the stream's name first, and both show the nats command.
