Overview answers "is everything healthy?" at a glance. Connections answers "who is connected, and who is slow?".

Overview

A bar at the top jumps between four sections and shows each one's status, so you can see what needs attention without scrolling:

  • Health: messages and bytes in and out per second, connections, slow consumers and JetStream storage, with a short live chart for each. Versions that differ across servers are flagged.
  • Servers: every server with its health, version, cluster, connections, CPU, memory and uptime.
  • Busiest streams: the top five streams by message count, linking to the full list.
  • Consumers with lag: the consumers furthest behind across all streams, with a lag bar.

The figures update live. The right of the bar says where they come from (System credentials or the Monitoring URL) and when they were taken.

Server-wide data

Health, Servers and Connections need either System credentials or a Monitoring URL on the Context. Without them, those parts say what's missing; stream and consumer data still shows. See Server-wide data.

Connections

The Connections screen lists every client connected to every server, with its name, account, user, server, round-trip time, pending bytes, messages in and out, and subscription count. You can:

  • Switch between open connections and recently closed ones, with the reason they closed.
  • Sort by any column, and filter by name, account, user or a subscribed subject.
  • Open a connection to see its subscriptions.
  • Kick a connection, after a confirmation. This needs System credentials, and is blocked in Read-only mode.
