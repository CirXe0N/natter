Live tail shows messages on a subject as they're published, like nats sub, but with a cap so a busy subject can't flood the window. It tells you exactly what it didn't show.

Start tailing

Type a subject in the filter, with * and > wildcards (orders.>), and press Enter. New messages appear at the top: time, subject and payload. Click one to see its headers and payload in the side panel. Natter keeps up to 64 MB of payloads and headers per tail; past that the oldest rows keep only their summary.

Controls

  • Pause stops the tail; Resume starts it again. Messages published while it was paused aren't shown, and Natter tells you how many you missed.
  • Clear empties the list.
  • Show rows containing… filters the rows already received by any text.
  • The copy button gives the matching nats sub command.

The status dot is green while live, amber when paused, and red when something went wrong.

Limits

The tail shows at most 1,000 messages a second and keeps the newest 10,000. The meter shows the current rate. If messages arrive faster, the badge counts how many were dropped by the rate cap, and separately how many the client's pending limit dropped. A disconnect is noted in the list.

Live tail only reads, so it works in Read-only mode.
